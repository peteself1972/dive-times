Tap a tile to stamp the time shown at its foot. Hold a tile, or tap ›, for full times and to correct them.

Limits & team

Times are hours:minutes. Due back = first in water + max in water + all time spent back in the bell. A bell visit counts as a break once it reaches the minimum break, and the break-due clock restarts when the diver goes back out.

Run log
